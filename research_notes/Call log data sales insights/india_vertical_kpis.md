# KPIs Sales Heads in Indian Telecalling-Heavy Verticals Manage To, and How Call-Log Data Maps to Them

Scope note: The research used about 15 web searches (Nov 2026). Direct page fetches to leadsquared.com and policyedge.in failed because the session's network allowlist does not include them, so several findings rely on search-result summaries of those pages. Almost all vertical benchmarks come from **vendor blogs, vendor case studies and job descriptions**. Treat every numeric target below as **indicative**, not an industry standard. Regulatory items (RBI, TRAI, IRDAI, DPDP) come from news, law-firm or regulator sources and are noted as such.

Call-log fields assumed available: rep, customer number, call type (incoming/outgoing/missed/rejected), timestamp, duration; optionally notes and lead status.

## Q1. What does a sales/telecalling head in each vertical review daily/weekly? (Vertical profiles: motion, funnel, KPIs, pain points)

### Takeaway
In every vertical the manager reviews the same activity layer: dials, connects, talk time, follow-ups due or overdue, and speed to first call. Each vertical then adds one "physical-world conversion event" that defines success: a site visit (real estate), a counselling session or admission (EdTech), a login or disbursal or a PTP (BFSI), an appointment (healthcare), a test drive (auto), a site survey (solar), a quote or booking (logistics), and an order (B2B distribution). Call logs measure the activity layer directly. The conversion event can only be measured if the rep sets a lead status or adds notes.

### Inferences
- The generic daily review across verticals is: dials, unique numbers reached, connects, connect rate, total talk time, average call duration, first-call SLA on new leads, follow-ups due/done/overdue, missed or unanswered inbound calls and callback time. The weekly review is stage conversion (lead→event→close) by rep and by source.
- Inbound-heavy verticals (healthcare, logistics via IndiaMART, auto enquiries, real-estate portal leads) care most about missed-call callback time and unanswered inbound rate. Outbound-heavy verticals (EdTech, loans, collections, solar) care most about dials, connect rate, talk time and attempts per lead.

## Q2. Published norms: calls/day, talk time, response SLAs, follow-ups, stage conversions (India)

### Takeaway
Published Indian norms cluster at **80–200 dials/day** and **2–4 hrs (up to 5 hrs) talk time/day** for outbound counsellor and telesales roles. The connect target appears as an absolute count (40–75 connects) or a percentage (80% of leads contacted). Response SLAs are usually framed as "within 5 minutes", which traces back to a US study, not Indian data. Stage conversion ratios are mostly vendor-sourced.

### Cited Findings
- General telecalling benchmark of about 80 calls/day/agent, described as a floor — [Callyzer telesales KPIs](https://callyzer.co/blog/telesales-kpis-every-telecalling-team-must-track/)
- Regional variation: average 68 calls/agent/day in Indore vs 92 in Mumbai (vendor data) — [Callyzer WFH telesales regional benchmarks](https://callyzer.co/blog/wfh-telesales-performance-india/)
- EdTech JD ranges: 100–200+ dials, 40–75+ connects, 2–5 hrs talk time — sources listed under Q1/EdTech ([ISHR](https://careers-page.com/ishr/job/L47V3WWV), [upGrad Abroad](https://career.io/job/senior-admissions-counselor-pune-upgrad-abroad-a247da56388a1c11a165278799b97b67), [CollegeDunia](https://jobs.weekday.works/career-counsellor-at-collegedunia-4419805132), [TeamLease](https://www.teamlease.com/jobs/academic-counsellors-jobs-in-delhi-gurgaon-veranda-race-670458))

Other sources restate different multipliers (391%, 9×) — [Sierra Interactive](https://www.sierrainteractive.com/insights/blog/speed-to-lead-real-estate/); [amorealty MIT study](https://amorealty.com/blog/mit-100x-study-why-speed-to-lead-matters); [techimply India lead response](https://www.techimply.com/blog/lead-response-time-sales)

- Solar: 70–80% of booked surveys get visited — [edesy solar](https://edesy.in/blog/ai-calling-for-solar-installers-lead-to-site-survey-2026)
- Auto: illustrative 6% conversion on test-drive enquiries — [CallSphere](https://callsphere.ai/blog/smb-india-automotive-dealership-service-ai-voice)

### Inferences
- A talk-time target of 2–4 hrs/day, divided by an average connected call of about 2–4 min, implies roughly 40–100 connected calls/day. That is consistent with the JD connect targets, so talk time and connects can be cross-checked from call logs.
- "Within 5 minutes" is the de facto aspirational first-call SLA in vendor content. A realistic Indian SLA for SIM-based teams is probably 15–30 min during working hours; this is an inference, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No authoritative Indian study (KPMG, EY, Redseer) on telesales productivity norms was found within the search budget.
- No sourced norm for the number of follow-up attempts per lead before marking it dead in India.
- No sourced norm for a missed-call callback SLA (e.g. 30 min) in any Indian vertical.

## Q3. Industry-specific nuances: lead sources, inbound/outbound mix, regulation (TRAI/DND, RBI, IRDAI, DPDP, recording consent)

### Takeaway
The key rules are:
- **RBI**: recovery calls are allowed only 8am–7pm (since a 2022 circular). New 2026 directions, effective 1 Jan 2027, reportedly add mandatory recording and six-month retention.
- **TRAI**: the TCCCPR 2nd Amendment (Feb 2025) bars promotional telemarketing from ordinary 10-digit numbers and assigns the 140 series to promotional calls and 1600 to service or transactional calls.
- **IRDAI**: distance-marketing guidelines require trained tele-callers, recording disclosure and a voice copy on request.
- **DPDP**: the Rules 2025 (notified Nov 2025) phase in consent and notice duties through 2026–27.

For SIM-based teams, call logs become compliance evidence as well as a productivity tool: call-time windows, attempt frequency and recording coverage can all be audited from them.

### Cited Findings
- **RBI recovery calling hours**: the Aug 2022 RBI direction says borrowers should not be called before 8am or after 7pm. It applies to commercial banks, RRBs, SFBs, NBFCs (incl. HFCs), co-op banks and ARCs — [Business Standard 2022](https://business-standard.com/amp/article/finance/rbi-directs-loan-recovery-agents-not-to-intimidate-borrowers-no-calling-before-8am-after-7pm-122081201144_1.html); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/business/rbi-directs-loan-recovery-agents-not-to-intimidate-borrowers-no-calling-before-8am-after-7pm-1135618.html)
- **RBI 2026 directions**: reportedly issued Aug 2026 and effective 1 Jan 2027. Calls and visits ordinarily only 8am–7pm unless the borrower requests otherwise; calls recorded and retained for at least 6 months. The draft stage proposed telling the borrower the call is recorded. Secondary coverage only; verify on rbi.org.in — [Exotel RBI loan recovery guidelines](https://exotel.com/rbi-loan-recovery-guidelines); [Upstox draft coverage](https://upstox.com/news/business-news/financial-regulations/rbi-proposes-overhaul-of-loan-recovery-norms-draft-rules-ban-harassment-cap-calls-to-8am-7pm/article-189398); [PolicyEdge](https://www.policyedge.in/p/rbi-sets-new-rules-for-loan-recovery-calls-agent-visits-and-phone-locking-from-2027) (not fetched)
- **TRAI TCCCPR 2nd Amendment (12 Feb 2025)**: 140 series kept for promotional calls; new 1600 series for transactional and service calls; telemarketers barred from ordinary 10-digit numbers; higher financial disincentives on access providers; complaint window extended from 3 to 7 days — [TRAI PR 11/2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf); [JSA Prism](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/JSA-Prism-Telecommunications_February-2025_TCCCPR-amendments.Final_.pdf); [Ensure IAS](https://www.ensureias.com/blog/current-affairs/trai-tightens-rules-to-curb-spam-calls-and-text-messages)
- A digital consent-management pilot with RBI and banks was launched in June 2025 — [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/?p=43797)
- **IRDAI distance marketing** (2011 guidelines, still referenced on IRDAI's policyholder site):
  - Tele-callers must be trained on the IRDAI syllabus.
  - Customers must be told the call is recorded and can request a voice copy.
  - Calling hours follow TRAI/DoT orders.
  - Single-premium ULIPs above ₹1 lakh may not be solicited by phone.
  - Sources: [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/story/irda-issues-new-guidelines-on-telemarketing/1/14582.html); [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/irda-issues-guidelines-telemarketing.html); [policyholder.gov.in distance marketing](https://policyholder.gov.in/en/distance-marketing)
  - No IRDAI-specific "9am–9pm" window was found.
- **DPDP Rules 2025**: notified Nov 2025, with phased commencement (12-month milestone 14 Nov 2026; later provisions 14 May 2027) — [PIB](https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf); [AMS Shardul alert](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Regulatory-Alert-Enforcement-of-DPDP-Act-and-Notification-of-DPDP-Rules.pdf); [EY POV](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-in/pdf/2025/11/ey-india-dpdp-act-2023-and-rules-2025-pov.pdf). Vendor interpretation: TRAI governs the right to place the call, DPDP governs the right to retain and process the recording, and each purpose needs separate consent — [Caller.Digital DPDP vs TRAI](https://caller.digital/blog/dpdp-vs-trai-consent-voice-recordings-audit-trail-india-2026)
- **Lead sources**:
  - Real estate: portals and ad forms send the same enquiry to several agents ([Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/blog/speed-to-lead-real-estate-2026)).
  - Logistics/B2B: IndiaMART/JustDial dominate. IndiaMART offers Push and Pull APIs for real-time lead capture ([Kylas](https://kylas.io/blog/capture-indiamart-leads-automatically-in-crm); [SimplyHired logistics JD](https://www.simplyhired.co.in/job/UShdz9MZzoq_K6NagsF2clSIS0pM3p2JgQdXcP9kDVAKBuW3NVB87w)).
  - Healthcare: patients ignore unknown numbers, which lowers outbound pickup ([MyOperator Prime IVF](https://myoperator.com/prime-ivf-centre-case-study)).

### Inferences
- Collections is the vertical where SIM call logs carry regulatory value. Calls outside 8am–7pm and repeated calls to the same number in a day can be flagged automatically from timestamps.
- The TRAI rule that bars 10-digit numbers for promotional telemarketing is a real tension for SIM-based telecalling. Calling leads who enquired themselves (service or follow-up calls on inbound leads) is the common practice. Whether a given call is "promotional" needs legal review. Call logs can help show that the number first contacted the business (an incoming call or form lead before any outgoing call), which helps evidence consent. This is an inference; not legal advice.
- Insurance and collections need recording coverage. "% of calls recorded" is a KPI that call-log plus recording tools can report.

### Gaps
- The official RBI 2026 direction text was not retrieved (the PolicyEdge fetch was blocked), so recording and retention details are from secondary sources.
- The text of the TRAI regulation on whether the 10-digit restriction applies to individual sales reps calling existing enquirers was not reviewed.
- The current IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests) 2024 master circular provisions on telemarketing were not checked.

## Q4. Which 5–8 call-log-derived KPIs are most valuable per vertical?

### Takeaway
Six KPIs are universal and fully measurable from SIM metadata: speed to first call, connect rate, talk time, missed-call callback time, follow-up adherence and attempts per lead. Vertical-specific outcome KPIs (site visit, test drive, PTP, appointment) need a lead-status or notes field. Call logs can still proxy some of them, e.g. a long connected call followed by a short confirmation call on or near the event date.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences (proposed mapping; the author's synthesis, not sourced benchmarks)
Legend: **D** = directly measurable from call metadata; **P** = proxy from metadata patterns; **S** = needs lead status or notes.

**Universal core (all verticals)**: dials/day (D); unique numbers dialled (D); connect rate = outgoing calls with duration > 0 (or > 10s) ÷ outgoing calls (D); total talk time per rep per day (D); average connected-call duration (D); first-call delay from lead creation or first incoming call (D if the lead timestamp or first incoming call is known); missed/rejected inbound calls not called back and callback lag (D); follow-ups overdue (S, or P via "no call to the number in N days").

**Per vertical (5–8 KPIs each)**
1. **Real estate**
   - Time to first call on new portal/ad leads (D/P)
   - Attempts until first connect (D)
   - Connects with talk time > 2–3 min, a proxy for qualified conversations (P)
   - Missed-call callback within 30 min (D)
   - Site visits scheduled per rep per week (S)
   - Day-before/same-day site-visit confirmation call made (P: a short outgoing call to that number within 24 h of the visit date)
   - Post-visit follow-up within 24 h (P/S)
   - Dormant-lead reactivation calls (D)
2. **EdTech/admissions**
   - Dials/day vs target 100–200 (D)
   - Connects/day vs target 40–75 (D)
   - Talk time vs 2–4 hrs (D)
   - Lead velocity, i.e. time to first call (D)
   - Long counselling calls > 10 min as a proxy for counselling sessions (P)
   - Follow-up adherence per lead (P/S)
   - Missed inbound callbacks during the admission-season peak (D)
3. **BFSI lending/cards/insurance**
   - First call on new leads (D)
   - Connect rate (D)
   - Talk time (D)
   - Calls per lead before login (P/S)
   - Document-collection follow-up cadence (P)
   - Login/disbursal per rep (S)
   - Insurance: % calls recorded or disclosed (requires recording)
4. **BFSI collections**
   - Calls outside 8am–7pm, the compliance breach count (D)
   - Calls per borrower per day/week (D)
   - Connect rate as a proxy for RPC (P; true RPC needs S)
   - Talk time per account (D)
   - PTP captured and PTP kept (S)
   - Follow-up call on the PTP date (P)
   - Bucket-wise attempt coverage (S for bucket)
5. **Healthcare**
   - Missed-call rate and missed-call callback time (D)
   - Inbound answer rate during OPD hours (D)
   - Unknown-number pickup issue: outbound connect rate (D)
   - Appointment confirmation/reminder calls made the day before (P)
   - No-show recovery calls (P/S)
   - Repeat-caller rate as a proxy for unresolved queries (D)
6. **Automobile**
   - Time to first call on enquiries (D)
   - Enquiry follow-up attempts within 7 days (D)
   - Test drives booked (S)
   - Post-test-drive call within 24 h (P)
   - Service-reminder call coverage before the due date (P, if the due list is known)
   - Missed inbound callback (D)
   - Connect rate per CRE (D)
7. **Solar**
   - Time to first call (D)
   - Connects with qualifying-length calls (P)
   - Surveys booked (S)
   - Day-before survey reminder call coverage (P), which protects the 70–80% visit rate
   - Post-survey quote follow-up cadence (P)
   - Subsidy-paperwork follow-ups (P/S)
8. **Logistics**
   - IndiaMART/JustDial lead first-call time (D)
   - Missed inbound callback (D), since inbound freight enquiries are time-sensitive
   - Quote follow-up attempts (P)
   - Repeat-shipper call frequency, i.e. account coverage (D)
   - Talk time per rep (D)
   - Enquiry → booking (S)
9. **Manufacturing/B2B distribution**
   - Dealer/retailer call coverage against the call list or beat (D: unique numbers called ÷ assigned list)
   - Call-cycle adherence, i.e. each account called every N days (D)
   - Orders taken per call (S)
   - Dormant-dealer reactivation (D: accounts not called or not calling in for 30 days)
   - Inbound dealer calls missed (D)
   - Average call duration per order call (D)
   - Tele-calls to outlets the field rep missed (P)

### Gaps
- No source validates specific duration thresholds (e.g. "> 2 min = qualified call") for India. Thresholds should be calibrated per customer.
- No source quantifies how accurately call-log proxies predict site visits, test drives or PTPs. This would need a customer-data study.
