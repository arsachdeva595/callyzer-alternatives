# Competitor Analytics Landscape: Reporting & Insights in Call/Sales-Engagement Tools (Global + India), 2025-2026

Method note: research done 2026-11-16 through web search (about 14 calls). Direct page fetches failed because these hosts are outside the session's network allowlist: callyzer.co, telecrm.in, vantagepoint.io. Most findings therefore come from search-result summaries of vendor pages, help docs, G2/Capterra and Play Store listings. Search tools paraphrase, so treat a quote marked "snippet" as wording seen in a search excerpt, not a check against the full page. Verify the most important quotes before publishing.

## 1. Global tools: dashboards, KPIs, automated insights, alerts, scorecards, AI (metadata vs transcript)

### Takeaway
Global tools fall into two layers. One is metadata dashboards (call volume, connect rate, talk time, missed or unanswered calls, leaderboards, best time to call), and these are usually included or sold as a modest add-on. The other is transcript-based AI (recaps, scorecards, sentiment, topic trackers, deal or revenue intelligence), which is a premium upsell. Almost every AI feature that gets marketed depends on recordings and transcripts. Metadata-driven automated insight is thin and mostly limited to dialer vendors (Orum's Best Time to Call, Kixie's per-list connect rate).

### Cited Findings
**Aircall**
- Third-party summaries list the standard Analytics tier as Overview, Monitoring and User Activity dashboards. Analytics+ adds Overview+, Inbound+, Outbound+, Unanswered Calls+, User Activity+, Monitoring+ and Network Diagnostics+, plus unlimited history, charts and heatmaps, customizable dashboards and advanced live monitoring. Without Analytics+, history in the dashboard is capped at 6 months. — [Quo: Aircall pricing](https://www.quo.com/blog/aircall-pricing/); [JustCall Aircall review](https://justcall.io/blog/aircall-review.html); [Squaretalk](https://squaretalk.com/aircall-pricing-explained/) (all METADATA)
- Analytics+ is a paid add-on, widely cited at about $15/license/month. One reviewer says Aircall won't confirm that price as current. — [Rezora](https://rezora.io/blog/aircall-pricing); [Hackceleration](https://hackceleration.com/labs/aircall-pricing)
- Aircall pricing page: Essentials $30 and Professional $50 per license/month. Professional "adds advanced analytics, smart routing, Power Dialer, and AI conversation intelligence." The AI add-on is cited at about $9/license, and one review cites AI Assist Pro at about $49/license. — [Aircall pricing](https://aircall.io/pricing/); [Quo: Aircall AI](https://www.quo.com/blog/aircall-ai/) (AI = TRANSCRIPT)

**Dialpad (Ai)**
- AI Scorecard analytics has three dashboards: Teams & Groups, Agents and Scorecards. It shows grades, graded interactions, performance changes and a leaderboard, with filters, call inspection and CSV export. The Teams & Groups view shows daily graded-scorecard counts and average grade against the previous 30 days. It is available only to Dialpad Support and Sell customers on Advanced and Premium plans, and only to admins or supervisors. — [Dialpad Help: AI Scorecard Analytics](https://help.dialpad.com/docs/AI-scorecard-analytics) (TRANSCRIPT)
- Dialpad markets grading of every conversation rather than a sample, with prebuilt and custom templates. — [Dialpad AI Scorecards](https://www.dialpad.com/ai-labs/ai-scorecards) (vendor claim)
- AI Recaps pulls the transcript, action items, highlights and notes into a call summary and can feed the CRM. — [Dialpad Voice Intelligence](https://www.dialpad.com/features/voice-intelligence) (TRANSCRIPT)

**RingCentral (RingSense, now "Conversational Intelligence" / "AI Conversation Expert (ACE)")**
- RingSense analyzes calls, meetings and messages for trends, sentiment and areas to improve, and produces post-call summaries and action items. Third-party sources cite about $60/user/month as an add-on on top of Core ($20) or Advanced ($25). The name has changed and sources disagree on details. — [Retell AI: RingCentral pricing](https://www.retellai.com/blog/ringcentral-pricing); [CloudTalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io/blog/ringcentral-pricing/); [Aloware](https://aloware.com/blog/ringcentral-pricing) (TRANSCRIPT)

**Kixie**
- The Kixie AI Dashboard shows calls, connected, connect rate, talk time, active reps and a leaderboard. For PowerDialer lists it shows lifetime dialed, answer rate and connect rate per list. — [Kixie Support: AI Dashboard](https://support.kixie.com/hc/en-us/articles/55213444516507-Kixie-AI-Dashboard-Introduction) (largely METADATA despite the "AI" name)
- Agent Status and Workforce Management covers metrics like average speed to answer, and there is a Business Snapshot report. — [Kixie Support: Agent Status](https://support.kixie.com/hc/en-us/articles/17465386328091-Agent-Status-Workforce-Mgmt-Overview); [Business Snapshot Report](https://support.kixie.com/hc/en-us/articles/17499874438683-Business-Snapshot-Report)

**Orum / Nooks (parallel dialers)**
- Orum's "Best Time to Call" feature "leverages call data to uncover the best times during the week to call prospects." — [Orum Analytics](https://www.orum.com/platform/analytics) (METADATA)
- Orum's own data: Mondays 8-9am and Wednesdays 5-6pm had the best connect rates (2023 data). A later post says connect rates peak at 10am local time. — [Orum cold calling 2025](https://www.orum.com/blog/cold-calling-2025); [Orum State of Cold Calling](https://www.orum.com/reports-and-guides/the-state-of-cold-calling) (vendor data)
- Orum claims a 5.3% baseline connect rate, rising to as high as 30% with Hot Numbers and Boost Connect. Vendor claim. — [Oliv.ai roundup](https://www.oliv.ai/blog/sales-engagement-platforms); [Orum vs Nooks](https://www.orum.com/reports-and-guides/nooks-alternative)
- I found little primary detail on Nooks' analytics. A competitor comparison says Orum has recording and manager analytics. — [Trellus](https://www.trellus.ai/post/orum-vs-nooks)

**HubSpot (Sales Hub calling)**
- The Call Analytics page and the "Call outcome totals by rep" report are available on Sales or Service Hub Starter, Professional and Enterprise. Custom outcome breakdowns reportedly need Pro or Enterprise. Report frequency goes down to "Daily" and no finer, so there is no native hourly reporting. — [HubSpot KB: call index](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/calling/review-calls-in-the-call-index); [Community: hourly call reporting](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/Dashboards-Reporting/hourly-call-reporting/m-p/538418); [Community: call dispositions](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/Dashboards-Reporting/Reporting-on-Call-Dispositions/m-p/537440)
- Long-running HubSpot Ideas threads ask for reporting on call outcomes and notes, which suggests a long-standing gap in native call reporting. — [Ideas: Reporting on call outcomes](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/HubSpot-Ideas/Reporting-on-call-outcomes/idi-p/15152); [Ideas: outcomes and notes](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/HubSpot-Ideas/Report-on-call-outcomes-and-notes/idi-p/17230)

**Gong / Salesloft (incl. Chorus) / Outreach**
- Gong is the reference product for conversation and revenue intelligence (TRANSCRIPT). G2 lists "Inaccurate AI Insights (37)" among its common cons. — [G2 Gong reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/gong/reviews)
- A blog estimate puts Gong Foundation alone at $1,400-1,600 per user per year. This comes from a single blog and is not verified. — [Gong review roundup (syncgtm / docket)](https://syncgtm.com/blog/gong-review)
- Comparison sources say Outreach is strongest on sequence and agent-level reporting and lighter on deal and forecast analytics. They describe Salesloft analytics as spanning cadence, conversations, deals and forecasting after its Clari tie-up. — [Oliv.ai](https://www.oliv.ai/blog/sales-engagement-platforms); [RevenueFlow](https://www.revenueflow.com/blog/sales-engagement-platforms) (secondary, partly competitor-authored)

**CallRail**
- G2's comparison says CallRail's dynamic number insertion and multi-channel attribution give it "an edge in providing comprehensive analytics for marketing efforts." — [G2 CallRail vs Calltouch](https://www.g2.com/compare/callrail-vs-calltouch-call-tracking) (attribution = METADATA; CallRail's Conversation Intelligence = TRANSCRIPT; not researched in depth)

### Inferences
- The "AI" label is applied loosely. Kixie's "AI Dashboard" is mostly metadata KPIs, so a metadata insight layer can credibly be positioned as smart or AI-assisted reporting.
- The automated, prescriptive insights these tools offer (scorecards, recaps, coaching) almost all require recordings and transcripts. A SIM-based app working only from call logs can't match them and should compete on metadata-native automated insight.
- Orum's best-time-to-call and Kixie's per-list connect rate show that metadata-derived recommendations are seen as valuable, though they are only offered inside dialers.

### Gaps
- Salesloft, Chorus (ZoomInfo), Outreach, CallRail and Nooks: no primary 2025-26 help-centre or changelog details captured, because of the limited tool budget and blocked fetches.
- Gong Labs material was not reviewed.
- Alerting (threshold or anomaly alerts) at global vendors was not specifically confirmed from primary docs.

## 2. Indian tools: missed-call alerts, follow-ups, lead response, scorecards, digests, AI summaries

### Takeaway
Indian telecalling CRMs (TeleCRM, Runo, NeoDove) and cloud telephony vendors (Exotel, MyOperator, Knowlarity, Ozonetel) all cover missed-call capture and alerts, follow-up reminders, agent reports and recordings. Runo is seen as the most reporting-oriented of the CRMs. I found no clear evidence of scheduled WhatsApp or email report digests, explicit speed-to-lead metrics, or AI-written insight narratives among the SIM-based CRMs. These look like open whitespace, though the absence isn't proven.

### Cited Findings
- NeoDove advertises instant notifications for missed calls and pending follow-ups, plus call reports, detailed user reports, hourly reporting, call recording and a WhatsApp API. — [NeoDove telecalling software](https://neodove.com/telecalling-software/); [NeoDove vs TeleCRM](https://neodove.com/alternative-to-telecrm/)
- Runo lists missed-call management, follow-up and callback notifications, and real-time agent monitoring with reports and scorecards. A comparison calls it the "reporting-first" pick. — [Runo features](https://runo.ai/features/telemarketing-crm-software); [RMDialer: Runo vs TeleCRM](https://rmdialer.com/blog/runo-vs-telecrm-comparison/)
- Runo pricing is cited at ₹199/user/month on a Runo-published list and ₹599/user on a third-party comparison. The sources conflict. — [Runo blog](https://runo.ai/blog/best-sim-based-calling-crm-software); [RMDialer](https://rmdialer.com/blog/runo-vs-telecrm-comparison/)
- TeleCRM features page (snippets): "Track scheduled, taken, and missed follow-ups"; "Are your agents busy or effective? Find out with agents' reports"; "Track calls, and WhatsApp to know who is initiating most conversations." I found no dedicated missed-call alert. — [TeleCRM features](https://telecrm.in/features)
- Exotel's developer docs describe a missed-call solution with auto-callback, a configurable callback delay and a deduplication window for repeat missed calls. Its call analytics include missed calls per agent and queue time per agent. — [Exotel Missed Call](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/call-support/call-features/missed-call); [Exotel Call Analytics](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/call-support/call-features/call-analytics)
- MyOperator markets a missed-call service that logs calls, alerts the team and runs auto-callback workflows to improve response time. A comparison blog says its analytics cover call counts, missed-call reports and basic response-time data. — [MyOperator Missed Call Service](https://myoperator.com/missed-call-service); [FasterQ comparison](https://www.fasterq.in/blog/myoperator-vs-alternatives-india)
- Ozonetel is described with agent performance dashboards, live monitoring, and answered, missed and waiting-time reporting. Knowlarity is described with missed-call numbers, callback queues and supervisor monitoring. — [FasterQ](https://www.fasterq.in/blog/myoperator-vs-alternatives-india); [Frejun](https://frejun.com/missed-call-service-providers-india/) (third-party)
- Zoho CRM telephony integrations can turn missed calls into follow-up tasks. Zoho Desk has a telephony dashboard with agent call counts and inbound and outbound volume. — [Techmagify](https://www.techmagify.com/blog/zoho-crm-integrations/); [Zoho Desk Telephony dashboard](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/desk/reports-and-dashboards/dashboards/articles/telephony-dashboard)
- Callyzer (Play Store): rated about 4.4 from 14.7K reviews in one snapshot and 4.6 from 7.12K in another. The Play Store says it only reads call logs for analytics. — [Callyzer on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.websoptimization.callyzerpro&hl=en_GB&gl=US)

### Inferences
- Missed-call capture and callback is table stakes in India. What sets vendors apart is measuring recovery (time-to-callback, the share recovered within an SLA), and only the cloud telephony vendors hint at that ("basic response-time data", "queue time per agent").
- Indian vendors offer "agent reports" and "scorecards" as activity counts. Transcript-based AI scorecards like Dialpad's are not evident among the SIM-based players.

### Gaps
- LeadSquared, Tata Tele Business, Salesforce India telephony and Ozonetel/Knowlarity: no primary docs on speech analytics, AI summaries or lead-response reports were found.
- WhatsApp or email report digests: no vendor-confirmed evidence for TeleCRM, Runo, NeoDove or Callyzer, because their sites were blocked or searches didn't surface them. Check each vendor's site directly.
- Callyzer's own current feature list and pricing couldn't be fetched (callyzer.co is blocked in this session).

## 3. Customer voice: praise and complaints about reporting and analytics

### Takeaway
Complaints cluster around four themes. Enterprise CI tools are overwhelming (too many trackers and dashboards, hard to customize). Reporting depth or granularity is missing (hourly views, per-contact roll-ups, long-period charts). Numbers are inaccurate or untrusted. Data has to be exported to get anything done. Praise goes to clean, easy-to-read, downloadable reports. I didn't retrieve any Reddit evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Gong (G2 snippets): the platform "can be a bit overwhelming for new users due to its rich set of features and data points"; filters "can be overwhelming or inconsistent"; "It can be difficult to fully customize reporting views or build flexible dashboards that match unique KPIs." — [G2 Gong reviews p3](https://www.g2.com/products/gong/reviews?page=3)
- Gong (Capterra snippets): "There are a lot of features available on the app that I definitely do not utilize"; "Very expensive standalone offering in the midst of a rising tide of competitors." — [Capterra Gong reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/157969/Gong-io/reviews/)
- A blog review says the number of Gong trackers and dashboards can bury the handful of insights a manager actually needs. — [Oliv.ai: Gong analytics](https://www.oliv.ai/blog/gong-analytics) (competitor-authored)
- Callyzer Play Store themes (search summary, paraphrased rather than verbatim): one user says reported durations and counts don't match their history. A consultant wants a per-contact total-call-time-by-day report for billing and says it's missing. Others complain about paid-tier and API support issues. Positive reviews praise the call-history analysis, the clean UI and the timing detail. — [Callyzer on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.websoptimization.callyzerpro&hl=en_NZ)
- Runo (Play Store, older review, paraphrased): the reviewer says the reports are misleading and relying on them could harm a business, and that support is very slow. Rated 4.3 from about 2.3K reviews. — [Runo on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.runo.call_crm&hl=en_IN&gl=US)
- TeleCRM (G2/Techjockey snippet): "the current reports are good, but I would love to see more advanced visual charts for tracking team performance over longer periods." — [G2 TeleCRM](https://www.g2.com/products/telecrm/reviews); [Techjockey TeleCRM](https://www.techjockey.com/reviews/telecrm)
- NeoDove: rated 4.8 from 931 Play reviews. Reviewers praise the reporting and ask for "Faster loading, more customization options". On G2, some users note occasional lags. — [NeoDove on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cellphone.neodove&hl=en_IN); [G2 NeoDove](https://www.g2.com/products/neodove/reviews)
- HubSpot users can't report more finely than daily, and outcome and notes reporting has been requested on the Ideas forum for years. — [HubSpot Community: hourly call reporting](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/Dashboards-Reporting/hourly-call-reporting/m-p/538418)
- Call tracking (G2, older reviews): ResponseTap: "not all data can be immediately exported / linked up to your other platforms ... you still need to log in and manually view/export reports"; CallSource: "the dashboard seems to be very cluttered"; CallTrackingMetrics: "no ability to label our scheduled exports." — [G2 ResponseTap](https://www.g2.com/products/responsetap/reviews); [G2 CallSource](https://www.g2.com/products/callsource/reviews); [G2 CTM](https://www.g2.com/products/calltrackingmetrics/reviews?qs=pros-and-cons)
- A vendor article on dashboards, attributing the point partly to RevOps communities, says most dashboards get ignored because they try to show everything to everyone. It cites Salesforce guidance of 3-4 metrics per goal. — [Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/sales-dashboard-examples) (secondhand)

### Inferences
- In both the enterprise and SMB segments, managers want fewer, trusted and focused insights rather than more reports. An "insight digest" (top 3 issues today) fits this demand.
- Accuracy and trust in call counts and durations is a real risk for SIM and call-log apps (dual SIM, OEM call-log quirks). An insight layer needs visible data-quality checks.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/sales, r/IndianStartups) and LinkedIn: searches returned no relevant threads, so I have no verbatim Reddit quotes.
- Several Play Store items are paraphrased by the search tool and should be checked against the live review text before quoting.
- I found no direct quotes specifically about "export-to-Excel culture" or "needing an analyst" in Indian SMB reviews.

## 4. Whitespace: metadata-only insights rarely offered (opportunities for Callyzer)

### Takeaway
Among the products reviewed, metadata-derived automated insights are offered narrowly: Orum's best time to call, Kixie's per-list connect rate, Exotel's missed-calls-per-agent and callback dedup, and MyOperator's basic response-time data. None of the Indian SIM-based CRMs appear to package the following as automated, proactive insights.

### Cited Findings
- Best-time-to-call exists only in dialer products (Orum) and is aggregate rather than per customer segment. — [Orum Analytics](https://www.orum.com/platform/analytics)
- Connect and answer rate per list exists at Kixie (per PowerDialer list). — [Kixie AI Dashboard](https://support.kixie.com/hc/en-us/articles/55213444516507-Kixie-AI-Dashboard-Introduction)
- Missed-call dedup and callback delay exist at Exotel as configuration, not as an SLA scorecard. — [Exotel Missed Call](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/call-support/call-features/missed-call)
- Response-time data at MyOperator is described as "basic." — [FasterQ](https://www.fasterq.in/blog/myoperator-vs-alternatives-india)
- HubSpot has no sub-daily (hourly) reporting. — [HubSpot Community](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/Dashboards-Reporting/hourly-call-reporting/m-p/538418)

### Inferences (opportunity list, all metadata-only, none confirmed as offered by Indian SIM-based competitors)
- **Speed-to-lead on new numbers:** time from a new unknown number's first inbound or missed call (or first CRM appearance) to the first outbound attempt, per rep.
- **Missed-call recovery SLA:** percentage of missed calls called back within X minutes, median callback time, and "never recovered" lists, delivered as alerts.
- **Attempts-to-connect:** dials until first connect per number, plus flags for under-attempted leads (e.g. fewer than 3 attempts) and over-dialed ones.
- **Duplicate or collision calling:** the same customer number called by several reps within a window (wasted effort, poor customer experience).
- **Best time to call by segment or region:** connect-rate heatmap by hour and day per tag or list, which Orum offers only in aggregate.
- **Short-call and fake-activity detection:** clusters of calls under 10 seconds, or patterns of dialing without connecting (activity gaming).
- **Silent-hours and idle gaps:** gaps in a rep's calling during shift hours.
- **Scheduled WhatsApp or email digests:** a daily or weekly "3 things to act on" summary instead of raw reports. Not evidenced at Indian competitors.
- **Data-quality and trust indicators:** these respond to accuracy complaints (Callyzer and Runo reviews).
- An LLM-written narrative over metadata (the "why" in plain language) can be built without recordings, which avoids the cost and consent burden of transcripts.

### Gaps
- I couldn't confirm the absence of these features at TeleCRM, Runo, NeoDove or LeadSquared from primary docs. Treat the whitespace as a hypothesis to validate through demos.
- No public benchmarks on speed-to-lead specific to the Indian SIM-calling context were found within the budget.

## 5. Pricing and positioning of analytics tiers

### Takeaway
Advanced analytics and AI are clearly sold as upsells globally. Aircall Analytics+ (about $15) and the AI add-on (about $9) sit on top of seat plans. RingSense/ACE costs about $60/user. Dialpad AI Scorecard analytics needs the Advanced or Premium plan. HubSpot custom call reports need Pro or Enterprise. Gong is enterprise-priced. Indian tools compete on low per-seat prices (Runo cited at ₹199-599), and I found no separate analytics tier.

### Cited Findings
- Aircall Analytics+ costs about $15/license/month (unverified). Standard history is capped at 6 months. Professional at $50 includes "advanced analytics." — [Aircall pricing](https://aircall.io/pricing/); [Quo](https://www.quo.com/blog/aircall-pricing/); [Rezora](https://rezora.io/blog/aircall-pricing)
- The Aircall AI add-on costs about $9/license, and AI Assist Pro about $49. — [Quo: Aircall AI](https://www.quo.com/blog/aircall-ai/); [JustCall](https://justcall.io/blog/aircall-review.html)
- RingSense/ACE costs about $60/user/month on top of $20-25 base plans. — [Retell AI](https://www.retellai.com/blog/ringcentral-pricing)
- Dialpad AI Scorecard analytics is limited to Advanced and Premium plans. — [Dialpad Help](https://help.dialpad.com/docs/AI-scorecard-analytics)
- HubSpot custom call outcome reports need Pro or Enterprise. — [HubSpot Community](https://community.hubspot.com/t5/Dashboards-Reporting/Reporting-on-Call-Dispositions/m-p/537440)
- Gong Foundation is estimated at $1,400-1,600 per user per year by a single blog. — [syncgtm](https://syncgtm.com/blog/gong-review)
- Runo is cited at ₹199/user/month and at ₹599/user, and the sources conflict. — [Runo blog](https://runo.ai/blog/best-sim-based-calling-crm-software); [RMDialer](https://rmdialer.com/blog/runo-vs-telecrm-comparison/)

### Inferences
- Common upsell levers are longer data history, more dashboards, custom reports, AI or transcript features and supervisor-only analytics. Callyzer could charge for an "Insights" tier (automated alerts, digests, SLA tracking, long history) while keeping basic call reports in the base plan. This matches global practice and suits price-sensitive Indian SMBs.

### Gaps
- Official current prices for Aircall Analytics+, RingCentral ACE and Gong weren't confirmed on vendor pages.
- Callyzer, TeleCRM and NeoDove tier pricing wasn't retrieved because their sites were blocked or not surfaced.
